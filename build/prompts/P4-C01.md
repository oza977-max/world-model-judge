# Build Prompt: P4-C01 — Judge types and verdict assembly

**In plain words:** the judge is the product, so its two doors have to be built so they
cannot be misused. The **input door** (`JudgeInput`) can only carry predictions, outcomes,
the two baselines, region labels, the world's drift curves, the climatology table, the tasks
and the pre-registered thresholds — there is no field for a model's name, its fixture flag,
its architecture or its training history, and nothing can be added to it, so the judge is
blind *by construction* (JU-1). Without both baselines it cannot even be constructed
(MU-2). The **output door** (`Verdict`) cannot exist unless all nine required groups are
present, correctly keyed and finite — a partial verdict is impossible to hold, so no code
can emit one (JU-9). The seven honest statements about the judge's own limits are fixed
text in one place and appear, word for word, in every verdict (JU-10). This chunk builds
those doors; the arithmetic that fills the blocks comes in P4-C02 to P4-C05.

## Chunk Summary
From `specs/implementation-guide.md` v2.0 P4-C01 and judge spec §4 (JudgeInput), §5 (the
Verdict record, the canonical keying rule, the nine groups), ADR-J7 (the seven disclosures
verbatim), §7 (error handling). Tests: TC-JU1-01 (blindness by type), TC-JU9-01
(nine groups), TC-JU9-02 (refuse when one is missing — a mutation/phantom-gate test),
TC-JU10-01 (seven disclosures verbatim), TC-MU2-01 (refuse without baselines). TC-JU9-03
(`exceptions.observed == sum(trials.is_exception)`) is owned by P4-C04, but the assembly
here enforces it too, so a verdict whose two blocks disagree cannot exist.

## The rules built (judge spec §5)
- The Verdict has exactly: `schema`, `world`, `skill`, `error_vs_horizon`, `calibration`, `sharpness`, `exceptions`, `trials`, `climatology`, `trust_horizons`, `not_tested`, `limitations` — and **no** `model_ref`, `model_name`, `is_fixture` or `meta` (those belong to the harness envelope).
- Canonical keying rule (table in §5): `skill.per_task_region` needs `task`, `region`; `error_vs_horizon.per_region` needs `region` (and the block carries `dt`); `calibration.per_task`, `sharpness.per_task`, `climatology.per_task`, `trust_horizons.per_task` need `task`, `region`; `exceptions.per_task` and `trials.per_task` need `task`, `region`, `horizon_step`. No field name may end in `_in`, `_out`, `_in_region` or `_out_region` (the banned suffix encoding).
- Every block present, non-empty, finite (no NaN/inf anywhere), JSON-able (numpy arrays/scalars are converted to plain lists/numbers); `limitations` equals the seven strings verbatim and in order; `exceptions.observed == sum(trials.is_exception)` for every key.
