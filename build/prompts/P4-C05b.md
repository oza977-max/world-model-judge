# Build Prompt: P4-C05b — The climatology table and the measured invariant bins (harness half of P4-C05)

**In plain words:** the judge's "does this still look like the world?" question needs a reference:
for each range of the world's conserved quantity (energy for the pendulum, the "orbit number" for
predator-prey), what states does the world actually visit, on average, and how widely? This chunk
builds that reference table — sixteen ranges holding equally many samples — and, for every test
trial at every step, works out which range the *true* trajectory is in at that moment (re-measured
at each step, because kicks move it). It also measures how well the true world agrees with its
own table, with and without kicks — the answer to owner question D15, "do the kicks upset the
reference?".

## Chunk Summary
From worlds spec §5 ("the conditioned-climatology table": 16 equal-population bins, outermost bins
open to ±∞, ≥ 50 samples per bin asserted) and judge ADR-J5, with the owner's 2026-10-14 ruling on
the spec defect below. Tests: TC-JU6-02 (re-measured per step, not frozen) — harness half;
D15 measurement.

## Spec defect found while building (owner-ruled, backlog A36)
The spec says: one continuous 200,000-step null-action trajectory per world *from the training
region's centre*. In both built worlds that centre is an equilibrium (predator-prey's fixed point,
the pendulum hanging at rest): the "trajectory" is one point, the conserved quantity never changes,
there are no sixteen bins and the spread is zero. **Ruled (owner, 2026-10-14): many seeded starts
across the training box.** 200 starts × 1,000 null-action steps = the same 200,000 samples; the
samples are pooled and cut into 16 equal-population bins by the conserved quantity. The module
refuses a degenerate reference (fewer than 16 distinct invariant values, a bin under 50 samples,
a bin with a zero spread) rather than hand the judge a table it would refuse.

## Conventions pinned here
- Starts: `seeds.rng_for(world, "training", "climatology-starts")`, uniform over the training box.
- Null actions only. Every step of every start is a sample.
- Bin edges: the 15 inner edges are the empirical quantiles `k/16` of the pooled invariant values (method "equal-population"); they must be strictly increasing; first bin's lower edge −∞, last bin's upper edge +∞. A bin is `[lo, hi)` — a value exactly on an edge belongs to the **upper** bin.
- `mean`/`sd` of a bin: the sample mean and population sd (ddof 0) of the states in it, per quantity.
- `invariant_bins`: for a `[n_trials, H, d]` array of true states, the bin of the conserved quantity of the state after each step (array index `s − 1` = step `s`), as `int64[n, H]`.
- Truth-agreement (D15): mean of `|state − bin mean| / bin sd` over trials, steps and quantities for true trajectories — what a perfect model would score on the judge's agreement test.
