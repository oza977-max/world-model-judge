# Handover: P4-C03 — the switch step, calibration and sharpness

**In plain words:** "90% sure" has to mean right 90% of the time. For each task and region the judge
counts how often the truth really lands inside the model's stated range, at four confidence levels
(50, 80, 90, 95%), counting a trial as covered only if *all* its quantities are inside. Beside
that it reports how wide the 90% ranges were — a model can be perfectly calibrated by shrugging, so
calibration is never shown without sharpness. Both are measured at one step per task: the step where
the world's own drift first exceeds that task's tolerance (the "switch step"), or the task's last
step when it never does. That second case is the usual one on the real worlds, which is recorded as
owner decision D20.
