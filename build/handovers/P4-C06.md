# Handover: P4-C06 — blindness and purity properties of the judge

**In plain words:** three promises about the judge, each proved by a test that can fail.
(1) *Blind:* it cannot tell which model it is judging — relabelling the regions, tasks or world,
reordering the trials, or reordering the regions' curves and tables changes nothing but labels and order.
(2) *Pure:* it is a calculation, not an activity — while it is imported, built from its input and run,
every known route to the clock, the files, the environment, the network, other programs and random
numbers is rigged to raise, and it finishes without tripping one, even by an error it tries to swallow;
and the rigging is proved to work by planting impure reads in a copy of the judge (one invisible to
the static source scan) and watching the rigging catch them. (3) *Not learned:* its source and imports
hold no weights, loaders, fitters or learning vocabulary.
