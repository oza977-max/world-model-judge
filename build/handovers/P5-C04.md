# Handover: P5-C04 — Comparison table, results page, writer

**In plain words:** the last reporting chunk is built. Three parts:

- **Chart 4, the comparison table.** Every model on a world gets one row:
  - its rank on ordinary error (control task, training region, one step ahead), with its skill against the two simple baselines beside it;
  - then its trust horizon for each task and region, in steps, world time and natural cycles ("—" where the world has none).

  Where ordinary error and the judge rank two real models in opposite orders, both rows are marked "⇄ disagree". The caption then names the pair:
  - "equals" only when the two are equal by the pre-registered matching margin;
  - otherwise "Ordinary error ranks A ahead of B; the judge trusts B further ahead on {task}, {region}".

  When no real pair disagrees, the caption says so: "Among the real models…" whenever fixture rows are shown. Fixtures are shown and tinted, never marked and never named.
- **The results page.** One static file with no scripts, opened from disk:
  - the model card near the top: the judge's ten limits and the never-tested list, word for word;
  - a note on what the purity check can and cannot catch;
  - each world's table and every model's charts, each with its caption.
- **The writer.** It is the only code that writes `out/`. It checks every envelope through the judge's own door, writes everything into a staging folder, then moves it into place in one step. Any refusal, or any failure part-way, leaves nothing.

## Retrospective
Each Important finding after the first pass was a sentence that could be technically true and still mislead: "equals" for distant models, "ranks B ahead" on one column of four, "agree" above a disagreeing fixture. A caption is a claim, and the test for it is "is this true of the table a reader sees?" — not "does it match the template". The property test the fourth reviewer ran, with an independent oracle over 1,500 random tables, is the right tool for that and would have caught all three at once. Next time, write that test with the first caption, not after three review rounds.
