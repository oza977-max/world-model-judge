# Handover: P4-C05 — error versus horizon, climatology agreement, trust horizons (the judge half)

**In plain words:** the last three numbers the judge computes. (1) *The error curve*: for each
region, the typical (median) distance between the model's best guess and what happened after 1,
2, 3 … steps, beside the world's own drift curve. (2) *Climatology agreement*: past the step where
the world itself has drifted beyond a task's tolerance, does the model's guess still look like the
world's long-run behaviour for the same amount of energy? (3) *The trust horizon*: the last step up
to which the typical error stayed within the task's tolerance at every step, never past the switch
step, in steps, in world time, and as a fraction of the world's natural cycle where it has one.
Failing even at step 1 is reported as 0.
