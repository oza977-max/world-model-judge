# Handover: P4-C01 — the judge's two doors (input types and verdict assembly)

**In plain words:** the judge is the product, so its two doors are built so they cannot be
misused. The input door (`JudgeInput`) can only carry forecasts, outcomes, the two baselines,
region labels, the world's drift curves, the climatology table, the tasks and the thresholds
fixed in advance — there is no field for a model's name, whether it is a deliberately broken
test model, its architecture or its training history, and nothing can be attached afterwards, so
the judge is blind by construction. The output door (`Verdict`) cannot exist unless all nine
required groups are present, correctly keyed, finite and consistent with each other, and it is
read-only once built. The seven honest statements about the judge's own limits are fixed text,
checked word for word, in every verdict.
