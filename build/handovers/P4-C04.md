# Handover: P4-C04 — exception counting and the pre-registered bands

**In plain words:** the banking-style backtest. Each of the independent test starts is a bet the
model made: "the truth will land inside my 90% range". The judge counts the misses ("exceptions")
and compares the count with what an honest 90% model would produce, and places it in a colour band
fixed before judging: for 200 starts, green is 12–29 misses (honest luck), amber is 8–11 or 30–35,
red is anything further out. Too many misses means over-confidence; too *few*, with very wide
ranges, is flagged as possible padding and never rewarded. Every task is counted at step 1 and at
the step where it is judged. The same per-trial hit/miss list the count is made from is what the
main chart draws, so the chart and the count cannot disagree.

## Deviations from Spec (backlog A32)
The chart's `band_hi` is the interval's radius, not its width (the plotted distance is measured from the mean); `is_exception` is the per-quantity joint test and is never re-derived from the two plotted numbers; the hedging flag fires for any count at or below the top of green when the judged-step mean width exceeds the mean of the (normalised) threshold vector; bands with no amber range on a side are refused.
