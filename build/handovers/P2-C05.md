## What this chunk delivers

In plain words: the project draws its first real chart from real
numbers. `wmj chart-preview` runs the predator-prey world forward,
holds the persistence baseline's "nothing changes" forecast against
it, measures how wrong that gets step by step, and plots it against the
world's own drift-apart curve — the floor below which the error is the
world's fault, not the model's. The chart is written to
`out/charts/lv-persistence-horizon.png` (and `.svg`), with a scoped
one-sentence-shorter caption in `out/captions/`.

This is a deliberately early thin vertical slice: it wires P1-C02's
CRPS/skill, P2-C03's divergence curve, the LV world's real
trajectories, the persistence baseline, and a minimal reporting layer
into one runnable command — twelve chunks before the judge's real
`error_vs_horizon` block exists (JU-3, P4-C05). The harness computes a
**stand-in with exactly the judge's block shape** (judge §5), so when
the real block arrives the renderer does not change — only the
producer swaps.

- `src/wmj/reporting/style.py` — the one shared 8×5 in @ 150 dpi style,
  fixed colour semantics (model dark blue, baselines grey, divergence
  black dashed, misses red, fixtures orange + labelled, ADR-R1),
  `mark_fixture` (ADR-R4), `save_figure` writing PNG + a byte-reproducible
  SVG with **both** §7 pins (`svg.hashsalt` and `metadata={'Date': None}`).
  Figures are built straight from `matplotlib.figure.Figure`, never
  through `pyplot` — no global state.
- `src/wmj/reporting/horizon_plot.py` — Chart 2 (ADR-R2): one panel per
  region, X from step 1 (index 0 is 0.0 by construction and `log(0)` is
  undefined — the *plot* drops it, the arrays are untouched), Y log for
  early-gap legibility (TC-RP2-01), world-time secondary axis = step ×
  dt, model blue solid, divergence reference black dashed, direct labels.
  A malformed block is **refused loudly** (`HorizonBlockError`), never
  drawn partially (reporting §7). Computes nothing (reporting §4).
- `src/wmj/reporting/captions.py` — `CHART2_FULL` (ADR-R3 verbatim) and
  `CHART2_SCOPED` **derived** as full-minus-its-switch-sentence, so the
  scoped caption cannot drift from the template; `write_caption` refuses
  an over-long caption (RP-5, ≤3 sentences).
- `src/wmj/harness/preview.py` — `build_lv_persistence_error_vs_horizon`
  (the judge-shaped stand-in block) and `one_step_skill_persistence_vs_linear`
  (the real CRPS/skill path, ADR-J1), plus `run_chart_preview` orchestrating
  producer → reporting.
- `src/wmj/harness/cli.py` — the internal-only `chart-preview` subcommand
  (ADR-R5), with `--n-starts/--n-trials/--horizon/--seed` so the
  acceptance test runs in seconds while the defaults are the spec's numbers.
- `src/wmj/models/baselines.py` — `fit_persistence_spread` made public
  with **`ddof=1`** (sample std) and a fail-loudly `DegenerateSpreadError`
  for a zero-variance or too-small training set; `persistence_factory`
  wired to it. The `ddof` choice changes bytes and is recorded as
  backlog A8.
- `src/wmj/harness/benchmarks.py` — `_declared_regions` → `declared_regions`
  (now a real consumer outside the module: `harness.preview`).

## Scope decisions carried through (from build/prompts/P2-C05.md)

- The scoped caption is the ADR-R3 Chart-2 template **minus its third
  (switch-step) sentence** — that sentence narrates `climatology.per_task`,
  first computed at P4-C05; the full verbatim caption and the switch
  lines arrive at P5-C03.
- Reporting computes no judgment metric (§4): the median-error
  computation lives in `harness/preview.py`; `horizon_plot.py` only
  validates and draws. Verified by the independent reviewer (no
  aggregation call in the reporting module).
- SVG bytes are reproducibility-claimed (both pins); PNG bytes are not
  (ADR-R5). The acceptance test asserts in-process SVG identity; the
  independent reviewer additionally confirmed **cross-process** byte
  identity already holds (the separate-process check is formally
  TC-RP7-02 at P5-C04).
