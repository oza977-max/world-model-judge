2026-10-06 · Round 2 (strict) · three panels (A+D references/naming, B+C contracts/logic, E concurrency/determinism); Panel F (stub detection) omitted — no STUBS.md in this project. Read-only review of src/wmj/models/*, harness training/sufficiency/prereg/kicks/action_response/derive_thresholds and their tests; includes the owed independent re-check of the D17/D18 amendments.

In plain words: the maths and the determinism on one machine held up under about 150 deliberate source breakages and many direct experiments. What the reviewers found is mostly the pre-registered recipe and the project's memory files saying things that are no longer true, one parser that would let a bad matching margin switch off a safety gate, and one honest limit: results are only byte-identical on the same kind of CPU.

Cleared by the reviewers (demonstrated)

  • β-NLL gradient, cosine decay (exact first and last rates), Adam, partial last batch, per-epoch shuffle, 64-pair gradient check, scaled-MSE, ensemble spread, every fixture corruption: correct, and about 150 mutants of them were caught.
  • D17: the quota is drawn kicks-first from the pool after the training set; the new stream shifts no other stream; held-out is disjoint. D18: median over 5 seeds uses run_seed+k, both sizes scored on the 2M held-out set, the M set is an exact prefix.
  • Same machine: call order, hash seed, locale, timezone and thread count never changed a byte; batch invariance verified over 2,820 combinations. Real-scale kick supply has about 4σ of margin on predator–prey.
  • prereg freeze-history walk and hardened git environment: 20 of 20 mutants caught. thresholds.json re-derived independently and byte-equal.
  • Confidentiality scan clean; no employer or internal names.
